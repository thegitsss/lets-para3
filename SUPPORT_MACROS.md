# Support response templates

These templates are starting points, not automatic promises. Before sending, verify the account, Matter, payment, and review state from authoritative LPC/Stripe records. Do not promise a resolution time. Never ask a user to send a full card number, bank details, credentials, authentication codes, privileged communications, or client-confidential documents by email.

## Payment attempt failed

Subject: Help with your Matter payment

Hi [Name],

The payment attempt did not complete. Open **Payments** and confirm that the saved payment method is current, then retry from the affected Matter. If Stripe requests additional authentication, complete that step in Stripe's hosted flow.

If the problem continues, reply with the approximate time, the Matter title or LPC Matter ID, and the error shown. If LPC already displays the card's last four digits, you may include those four digits only. Do not send a full card number, security code, bank information, password, or authentication code.

## Review request opened

Subject: Review request received

Hi [Name],

We recorded your review request for [Matter]. The affected workflow remains in its displayed review state while the team examines the available records. You can add a concise factual explanation through the authorized Matter or support workflow. Do not email privileged communications or client-confidential files.

We will update the recorded request when its status changes. LPC does not promise a fixed resolution time.

## Attorney onboarding guidance

Subject: Getting started as an attorney

Hi [Name],

The usual path is:

1. Complete your profile.
2. Add a payment method in **Payments**.
3. Create a Matter with a clear scope, deadline, and tasks.
4. Review applications or invite a Paralegal, then confirm the hire through the Matter workflow.

If a step is unavailable, send us the page, action, and exact error shown so we can check the recorded state.

## Paralegal onboarding guidance

Subject: Getting started as a paralegal

Hi [Name],

After your application is approved, the usual path is:

1. Complete your profile and availability.
2. Complete Stripe payout setup.
3. Browse open Matters or review invitations.
4. Apply or respond through the relevant Matter workflow.

If a step is unavailable, send us the page, action, and exact error shown so we can check the recorded state.

## Reduce email notifications

Subject: Update your email notifications

Hi [Name],

Open **Account Settings**, then use **Email notifications** and the message/Matter email controls to choose which optional notifications you receive. Security, legal, payment, or other essential service notices may still be sent when required.

If a saved preference is not being honored, reply with the notification type and approximate delivery time so we can investigate. LPC does not currently offer a custom email cadence.
