## Implemented

- Terms and Privacy describe the public site, directory and aggregate map, Attorney and Paralegal workflows, pre-engagement checks, locked scope, payment/review outcomes, records, authentication, and account/Matter Assistant processing. Role and release-dependent features are not represented as universally deployed.
- Required confidentiality now applies before and during every engagement, including outside tools, subcontractors, incident reporting, retained copies, and protected reporting exceptions. Additional NDAs remain available.
- New engagements have a default assignment of original paid work product, a license for incorporated pre-existing materials, rights to use delivered work while payment is pending, and protection for original client files. Earlier ownership arrangements are not retroactively replaced.
- Indemnity addresses third-party claims caused by specified user conduct, with defense and settlement procedures. Liability, notice, venue, jury/class provisions preserve nonwaivable rights. No arbitration regime was added. The commercial liability cap remains subject to counsel review.
- Privacy requests include agents, verification, explanations of denials, appeals, and nonretaliation where applicable. Acknowledgement is separate from consent. Changes do not silently authorize new uses of previously collected information.
- Registration requires two affirmative choices and the exact document fingerprints displayed. Existing members continue under ordinary authorization without an updated-policy review gate. Historical policy copies remain readable; the retired `legal-review.html` alias returns safely to the permitted destination or login.
- `LegalDocumentVersion` retains the exact public HTML with a deterministic identifier derived from document type and SHA-256. User receipts retain versions, hashes, acceptance time, source, and session identifier. Same-date document corrections trigger review. Legacy timestamps survive a first updated acceptance without inventing missing historical document hashes.
- Signup records acknowledgement of the exact documents presented. Existing-member postings/publications, relisting, applications, invitations, pre-engagement and hiring retain their substantive authorization and readiness rules without a policy-update acknowledgement prerequisite. Existing obligations and records remain accessible under ordinary authorization.
- Signup safeguards retain explicit choices, exact document fingerprints and applicable request/identity protection. No automatic or fabricated existing-member acceptance is introduced. The earlier updated-policy writer/idempotence results below are historical rather than required current endpoints.
