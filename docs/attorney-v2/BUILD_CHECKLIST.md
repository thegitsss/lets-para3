3. Keep **local implementation**, **full product acceptance**, **production rollout**, and **V1 retirement** as separate milestones. Most of the latter three are still pending.
4. Report completed capabilities and remaining gates, not a percentage calculated from unequal phase counts. Do not call the attorney rebuild complete while ordinary workflows merely open V1.
