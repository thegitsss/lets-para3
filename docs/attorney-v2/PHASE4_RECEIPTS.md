# Phase 4 attorney receipts

Recorded September 6, 2026. Bounded continuation of P4-03, with an early dependency on P8-04. The owner authorized continued attorney work and requires specific legal-work language and restrained LPC presentation under [EDITORIAL_STANDARD.md](EDITORIAL_STANDARD.md).

## Implemented behavior

Both attorney dashboards now offer **View receipt** from a published Matter. The current dashboard opens its existing dialog; V2 opens `#/matters/:caseId/receipt`. Both use the same review controller. The screen presents the Matter, receipt type, payment or payout status, recorded date, attorney, receipt number, currency, amounts and payment method in a plain definition list. An available receipt has an explicit download action. Unavailable payment evidence is explained without fabricating a paid amount or a receipt date.

The review clears previous financial details during refresh and on errors or access loss. Downloads can be canceled. Closing the current dialog, navigating away or changing accounts discards late responses. A changed financial revision clears the old review and requires a fresh review before another download. The browser validates the response type and PDF signature, checks the account again after receiving the bytes, and reports that the download was requested; it does not claim the file was saved to disk. Receipt details and PDF bytes are not persisted in browser storage.

This is one currently applicable document per Matter, following the existing payment-versus-finalized-withdrawal selection. It is not the full Payments screen, a complete receipt history, a CSV statement, or a reconstruction of multiple past withdrawals. Those remain in Phase 8.

## Financial authority and compatibility

`backend/services/attorneyReceipts.js` reads raw Case and User records without normalization or repair writes. It requires the approved current attorney, consistent owner aliases, current account status/auth version and an active managed session when present. BSON and historical text owner references remain supported. Ownership and financial inputs are checked again after external reads and PDF generation.

For a payment receipt, the stored unique payment-intent pointer must resolve to matching current processor evidence. The captured amount received, captured charge amount and currency must agree with the original Matter amount and attorney fee. Conflicting present processor ownership metadata, customer, transfer group, mode, duplicate intent pointers, disputed charges and inconsistent evidence prevent issuance. Historical records without modern metadata or a nonzero stored fee snapshot retain compatibility when the actual capture and historical fee calculation agree. Intended payment amounts and Case status alone never prove that money was received.

Refund evidence is read completely, with 100 records per page and a conservative ten-page cap. Duplicate, incomplete, malformed or inconsistent results require review. Processed refunds reduce the displayed remaining amount. Pending refunds appear separately and are not subtracted until processed. Failed refund attempts are distinguished from successful refunds. The receipt preserves the original funding amount instead of substituting a later remaining balance or dispute release amount. Missing historical payment dates and card details remain explicitly unavailable.

For the existing finalized-withdrawal branch, the Payout ledger is the authority. A positive withdrawal needs a paid, unreversed ledger entry with a matching paralegal, transfer reference and actual net amount. The fee is the difference between the finalized gross withdrawal amount and that recorded net payout. The document says the payout was recorded and that bank arrival is not confirmed. A recorded zero decision produces an explicit zero-payout receipt. Pending, failed or reversed ledger states cannot produce a paid withdrawal receipt. This path makes no incoming-funding Stripe call.

Case money fields are stored in cents. Receipt issuance requires a recognized monetary currency whose standard minor unit is two decimal places; other units require review instead of silent reinterpretation. Historical omitted currency defaults to USD and still must match the captured payment currency.

## API and shared changes

- `GET /api/payments/receipt/attorney/:caseId/review` requires `expectedOwnerId` and returns the bounded financial projection plus its revision, or a named unavailable reason.
- The existing `GET /api/payments/receipt/attorney/:caseId` remains available to existing callers. The shared current/V2 review sends the expected owner and reviewed revision. Old direct links may omit those query parameters but still receive fresh server-side eligibility and ownership checks.
- Attorney receipt responses use private/no-store caching. Downloads generate a fresh PDF instead of serving an old cached paid/refund document. No receipt cache is written and no payment, refund, transfer, notification or lifecycle mutation is performed.
- Evidence is re-read after PDF generation; a changed account, ownership, amount, refund state or withdrawal ledger prevents delivery. Separate database/provider reads are not an atomic financial snapshot, so a change after the final check cannot be ruled out by these checks. Provider latency and concurrent PDF-generation load still need release verification; mocked providers do not measure those costs.
- The shared receipt renderer now escapes every detail value and the receipt ID, supports an explicit date label, wraps long names, and reserves a real printed footer margin. Line items and payment status stay together where they fit. This corrects an HTML interpolation issue and footer overlap affecting shared receipt generation. Existing paralegal receipt access and financial builders remain in place.
- The release marker is `attorney-v2-receipts-20260906`; the remote cohort remains disabled with an empty attorney list.
