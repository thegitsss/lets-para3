### Concrete quality measures

- No known broken action, wrong destination, misleading financial/permission state, lost draft, inaccessible critical control or material unreviewed visual regression on an intended journey.
- No new document-level overflow, control overlap, duplicate semantic announcement or layout jump caused by ordinary loading/navigation at supported widths.
- Field results are retained after validation/network failure; confirmed changes survive refresh; privacy boundaries hold after account switch and access loss.
- Existing source-defined performance budgets pass on the final candidate. Industry reference thresholds are LCP <= 2.5 seconds, INP <= 200ms and CLS <= 0.1 at the 75th percentile, segmented by mobile/desktop. Prelaunch lab results and measured production field results are recorded separately; 28 days of representative field data cannot be invented before users exist.
- Test critical journeys in the pinned Chromium/Firefox/WebKit engines and on the real browser/device set in [BROWSER_SUPPORT.md](BROWSER_SUPPORT.md). Engine emulation does not substitute for actual Safari/iOS/Android/Edge acceptance.
- A complete first-use journey and a returning-user daily-work journey can be completed with the interface's own wording and controls. Record every dead end, unexplained choice, unnecessary detour and repeated entry as a finding to resolve; do not ask the owner to discover these during piecemeal review.

### Scope of the 2026 benchmark

These are LPC-specific acceptance criteria informed by current primary references, not a claim that LPC now matches another product. Stripe's app guidance emphasizes reusable components, consistent accessible presentation and object-contextual workflows. Linear describes fast interaction through both visible navigation and keyboard/command-menu access. Neither reference makes adding a particular framework, a chat-first interface, mandatory AI, native apps, time tracking, a recommendation score, a new board view, or every conceivable productivity feature a launch requirement.

Optional additions from older audits (for example external calendar subscriptions, saved-search alert subscriptions, visual document diffing and workload planning) remain separately identified product enhancements unless an existing accepted requirement already includes them. Their absence is not automatically a confirmed defect. Existing accepted features and the quality requirements above remain in scope; this distinction prevents an endless expanding feature list.

After authorized deployment: append live verification matching that approved candidate before recording production GO. The initial review package contains the live-check procedure and pending status, not invented post-deploy results. These are approvals and operational verification of finished work, not owner exploratory QA. |

If any required item fails, reopen its workflow, preserve the failure evidence, correct it and rerun the appropriate checks. A new candidate must complete the final required aggregate. Do not hide a product failure by retries, weakened assertions, mock success, fabricated empty states or deleting the affected UI capability.

"Ready for final review" means the intended product is complete and required predeployment verification is supported by evidence; it is not a claim that pending production-deployment checks have passed. "Launched and verified" additionally requires the authorized deployed version to pass its live checks. Neither phrase is a promise that software can never have a future defect; both are concrete, auditable decisions rather than guesses or percentages.

### Assessment limits

This expanded inventory used current source, the latest local implementation records, prior acceptance/hardening evidence, targeted read-only reproductions and current primary design/accessibility/performance references. It did not implement the remaining product features, rerun the full release suite, visually inspect every current screen, verify all supported real devices, contact live payment/storage providers or deploy LPC. The remaining integration, visual and external rows are open for those reasons; they are not claims that all underlying systems are broken.
