Attorney FAQ

For attorneys

Let’s-ParaConnect is a software platform designed to help solo and small-firm attorneys engage independent paralegals for clearly scoped matters—without staffing agencies, full-time hiring commitments, or administrative friction.

Account access is approval-based. LPC reviews submitted application information before deciding whether to grant platform access; approval is not independent credential verification or an endorsement.

Minimum Matter compensation is $400.

1) Vetted paralegals

Every paralegal completes LPC’s application and eligibility review before joining the platform. The profile contains information supplied by the paralegal about their background, practice areas, and matter experience. LPC does not independently authenticate credentials or guarantee qualifications. Attorneys remain responsible for evaluating experience and fit before engaging anyone.

2) Matter-based work—not placement

Paralegals independently review postings and apply to work that aligns with their experience. Let’s-ParaConnect does not place, match, or assign paralegals. Attorneys retain full discretion over who they engage and how work is managed.

3) Clear scope before work begins

Each Matter includes a task overview so expectations are aligned from the outset. Final task details and workflow are confirmed after a paralegal is hired, allowing flexibility while maintaining clarity and control.

4) Stripe-processed, approval-based payments

Attorneys fund Matter work through Stripe. Let’s-ParaConnect uses Stripe Connect to facilitate payment processing and route payouts to the paralegal connected to that Matter once completion is confirmed in the platform or a platform review is resolved. Let’s-ParaConnect charges a platform fee for use of the software platform and does not take custody of user funds.

5) Independent, professional engagement

Paralegals work as independent professionals, not employees of the platform. Attorneys maintain control over scope, communication, and deliverables, remain responsible for supervision and final work product, and are responsible for any required client consent. Unless permitted by applicable professional conduct rules, paralegals may not communicate directly with the attorney’s clients or others involved in the legal matter. Let’s-ParaConnect provides the infrastructure for documentation and workflow clarity, but does not assign engagements or require users to work together.

6) What Let’s-ParaConnect is — and isn’t

It is

  • A professional platform for clearly scoped matter work
  • Built specifically for solo and small-firm attorneys
  • Designed for experienced paralegals
  • Structured, transparent, and intentional

It is not

  • A law firm
  • A staffing agency
  • A placement service
  • A job board
  • An open or public sign-up platform

Let’s-ParaConnect is a software platform that enables attorneys to engage independent paralegals and facilitate payments through Stripe Connect. Let’s-ParaConnect is not a law firm, does not provide legal advice, does not assign engagements, and is not an escrow service, money transmitter, or payment processor. Using LPC does not create an attorney-client relationship with LPC. LPC is not a channel for attorneys to communicate with their own clients and must not be used to receive, transmit, or hold client trust funds.

Let’s-ParaConnect is a private, approval-based platform. Admission is limited and granted by approval only.

7) Is there a minimum Matter amount?

Yes. Matters posted on Let’s-ParaConnect have a minimum compensation amount of $400. The minimum is a posting requirement, not a guarantee that a Matter is adequately scoped or that a particular engagement will be successful.

8) Are there platform fees?

Yes. For an initial hire, Let’s-ParaConnect charges the Matter amount plus a 22% attorney platform fee through Stripe when the attorney confirms the hire. No separate Stripe processing line item is added beyond the total displayed at confirmation. The billing area and Matter receipt reflect the applicable Matter-specific amounts.

9) Can attorneys request conflict checks?

Yes. Before hiring, an attorney may optionally request pre-engagement items for a selected paralegal through LPC. These can include a confidentiality agreement and a conflicts check so the paralegal can review names, parties, or other matter details before hiring and funding move forward.

These pre-engagement items are optional tools within the platform. They are not automatically required on every matter, and attorneys remain responsible for supervising the engagement and handling any professional obligations that apply to the matter.

10) Admission & availability

Attorney accounts are reviewed manually, and platform access is limited to approved accounts.

Self-serve account deactivation is handled in Settings. It disables access and removes an account from active platform participation, but it is not full erasure. Attorneys cannot deactivate while involved in an active Matter or while funding, payout, dispute, or other financial obligations remain unresolved.

11) What do platform fees support?

Platform fees support LPC’s application and eligibility review, technology and matter workspace, platform support, applicable Stripe processing costs, and other platform operations. Platform fees are not fees for legal services.
