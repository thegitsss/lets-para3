Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to an accessible experience for all LPC users.

Last reviewed August 15, 2026

Our Accessibility Target

Let’s-ParaConnect is committed to making the platform usable by people with disabilities. Our current design and engineering target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA. This target guides our work; it is not a representation that every page or third-party surface has completed an independent conformance audit.

Our release checks include automated accessibility testing across supported browser engines, keyboard and focus checks for critical workflows, semantic structure checks, and contrast checks. Automated testing cannot identify every accessibility barrier, so reports from people using assistive technology remain important.

Third-Party Services

Some workflows open services operated by other companies, including Stripe. Those services maintain their own accessibility practices, and LPC may not control every part of their hosted experience. Please report a barrier even when it occurs on a linked service so we can investigate the handoff and provide available assistance.

Report an Accessibility Barrier

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of LPC, email help@lets-paraconnect.com or use the contact form. Include the page, the action you were trying to complete, your browser and assistive technology if relevant, and the barrier you encountered. Do not send passwords, payment-card data, or confidential Matter content by email.
