1) What does Let’s-ParaConnect do for paralegals?

Let’s‑ParaConnect is a technology platform where approved independent paralegals can connect with attorneys seeking support on clearly scoped matters. Attorneys and paralegals decide directly whether to work together. LPC’s workflow supports remote collaboration, and payments are facilitated through Stripe Connect.

2) Do I have to pay anything to join?

No. There are no subscription fees or upfront costs for paralegals. Let's-ParaConnect charges an 18% platform fee on completed, paid work—there are no fees until you earn.

3) How do payments work?

Attorneys fund a Matter through Stripe when they confirm the hire. After the required scope tasks are complete and the attorney marks the Matter complete, LPC releases your payout to Stripe. The 18% platform fee is deducted from the Matter compensation before release; check Stripe for the current payout status and estimated arrival.

4) Can I choose which Matters to apply for?

Yes. You have full control over which matters you apply to. If an attorney invites you directly, you may accept or decline based on your availability and area of expertise.

5) Do I need to be certified?

Formal certification is not required. Applicants must describe relevant legal-office or legal-support experience performed under attorney supervision, or identify relevant paralegal education or certification. LPC reviews the submitted application as described in Our Vetting Process; approval is not independent authentication of those qualifications.

6) How soon can I start working?

Once your application is approved and your profile is complete, you may begin applying to open matters. You can check your current application status by signing in.

7) Do attorneys see my resume?

Yes. Attorneys can review your résumé, listed skills, areas of specialization, and software proficiency when evaluating your profile for potential work.

8) How is Matter compensation handled?

Rates are set by the attorney and are currently offered on a flat‑fee basis. LPC enforces a $400 minimum posted amount. That minimum is a posting requirement, not a guarantee of scope, quality, or fit.

9) Can I receive repeat work from the same attorney?

Yes. Attorneys may re‑hire paralegals they’ve previously worked with directly through the platform.

10) What if there’s a dispute?

If there’s a concern about scope or completion, both parties can submit details through the platform’s review workflow before payment release. Let’s-ParaConnect may request supporting information, such as the agreed task list and completion notes, in order to administer the platform’s workflow and payment policies.

Self-serve account deactivation is handled in Settings. It disables access and removes an account from active platform participation, but it is not full erasure. Paralegals cannot deactivate while involved in an active Matter or while funding, payout, dispute, or other financial obligations remain unresolved.

11) What do platform fees support?

Platform fees support LPC’s application and eligibility review, technology and matter workspace, platform support, applicable Stripe processing costs, and other platform operations. Platform fees are not fees for legal services.
