Our Vetting Process

How LPC evaluates applications for admission to the platform.

Let’s-ParaConnect (“LPC”) is a technology platform that enables attorneys to evaluate independent paralegals and engage them for clearly scoped matters. Every paralegal must complete LPC’s application and eligibility review before joining the platform. Admission is approval-based and not guaranteed.

How Applications Are Reviewed

LPC reviews each submitted application as a whole. The review considers whether the application and required résumé are complete and internally consistent, whether the applicant meets LPC’s location and experience requirements, and whether the submitted information supports attorney-directed matter work. The profile is created after application approval and is not part of the vetting decision.

Minimum Eligibility Requirements

Applicants must be based in the United States and have at least one year of professional paralegal experience supporting attorneys. Relevant paralegal education or certification may be included and considered with the application, but it does not replace the one-year professional-experience requirement.

Application Materials

A PDF résumé is required with the application. A paralegal certificate or license may be included but is optional. LPC reviews the information and files submitted with the application; the standard vetting process does not independently authenticate credentials, contact former employers, query professional databases, or conduct a background check. Applicants are responsible for submitting accurate and complete information.

Reasons Applications May Be Declined

Applications may be declined when an applicant is not based in the United States, does not have at least one year of professional paralegal experience, submits incomplete or internally inconsistent materials, provides misleading information, or does not demonstrate alignment with attorney-directed matter work and current platform requirements.

Appeals and Feedback

Due to application volume and the approval-based nature of the platform, Let’s-ParaConnect does not provide individual feedback or an appeal process for admission decisions. All admission decisions are final.

Corrections After a Decision

There is no self-service appeal or reapplication workflow. If an admission decision relied on materially incorrect application information, the applicant may use the contact form to identify the correction. Contacting LPC does not guarantee reconsideration or approval.

Platform Discretion

Let’s-ParaConnect reserves the right to approve, deny, suspend, or revoke access to the platform at its sole discretion in order to protect attorneys, paralegals, and overall platform integrity.
