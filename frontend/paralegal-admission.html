Paralegal Admission Requirements

How LPC evaluates applications for its approved paralegal network.

Last updated August 15, 2026

Let’s-ParaConnect (“LPC”) is a technology platform that enables attorneys to review approved independent paralegal profiles and engage paralegals for project-based support work. Admission to the platform is approval-based and not guaranteed.

How Applications Are Reviewed

LPC reviews each submitted application as a whole rather than deciding on a single field such as years of experience, job title, or certification. Reviewers consider the legal-support experience described in the application, whether the résumé and profile are complete and internally consistent, and whether the submitted information supports attorney-directed project work. Approval is discretionary and depends on the application materials and current platform requirements.

Minimum Eligibility Requirements

Applicants must describe relevant legal-office or legal-support experience performed under attorney supervision, or identify relevant paralegal education or certification. The required résumé and profile should clearly describe the applicant’s experience and the types of attorney-directed work they have performed.

Application Materials

A PDF résumé is required with the application. A paralegal certificate or license may be included but is optional. LPC reviews the information and files the applicant submits; the standard admission review does not independently authenticate credentials, contact former employers, query professional databases, or conduct a background check. Applicants are responsible for submitting accurate and complete information.

Reasons Applications May Be Declined

Applications may be declined when the submitted materials do not describe relevant legal-support experience or education, are incomplete or internally inconsistent, contain misleading information, or do not demonstrate alignment with attorney-directed project work and current platform requirements.

Appeals and Feedback

Due to application volume and the approval-based nature of the platform, Let’s-ParaConnect does not provide individual feedback or an appeal process for admission decisions. All admission decisions are final.

Corrections After a Decision

There is no self-service appeal or reapplication workflow. If an admission decision relied on materially incorrect application information, the applicant may use the contact form to identify the correction. Contacting LPC does not guarantee reconsideration or approval.

Platform Discretion

Let’s-ParaConnect reserves the right to approve, deny, suspend, or revoke access to the platform at its sole discretion in order to protect attorneys, paralegals, and overall platform integrity.
