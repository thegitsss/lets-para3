Fees and minimums

Know what to expect from the start.

Platform fees.

To get started $0

Post for free.

Post a matter without adding a payment method. When you find the right paralegal, you’ll add one to hire them. Compensation for the paralegal’s work starts at $400 per matter.

For attorneys 22%

Added when you hire.

You set the matter amount. When you’re ready to hire, that amount plus LPC’s 22% platform fee is processed through Stripe. After you confirm all work for the matter is complete, Stripe releases payment directly to the paralegal.

For paralegals 18%

Deducted before payout.

The paralegal platform fee is deducted from the matter amount before payout, after completed work is approved.

Payments are securely processed by Stripe. LPC does not store full card details.

Common questions

Answers before you get started.

Does LPC assign a paralegal to an attorney?

No. Attorneys review applications and profiles, then decide whether and whom to engage.

Who defines the work and compensation?

The attorney defines the matter’s scope, deliverables, timing, and offered compensation.

Is Let’s-ParaConnect a law firm or staffing agency?

No. LPC is a technology platform. It does not provide legal advice, assign professionals, or supervise legal work.

How are payments handled?

The attorney funds the Matter through Stripe when hiring. LPC uses Stripe Connect to facilitate payment processing and route the payout to the paralegal after completion is confirmed in the platform or a platform review is resolved.

Can I request a conflicts check before hiring?

Yes. You can request a conflicts check or confidentiality agreement from a selected paralegal before hiring. These tools are optional; attorneys remain responsible for the professional obligations that apply to their Matter.
