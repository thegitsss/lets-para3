Fees and minimums

Know what to expect from the start.

What it costs.

To get started $0

Publish for free.

No payment method is required to publish. Add one securely through Stripe when you hire. Matters start at $400 in compensation for your paralegal’s work.

For attorneys 22%

Added when you hire.

The attorney platform fee is added to the matter amount when you hire a paralegal. Payment processing is included in the displayed total.

For paralegals 18%

Deducted before payout.

The paralegal platform fee is deducted from the matter amount before payout, after completed work is approved.

Platform fees support LPC’s application and eligibility review, technology and matter workspace, platform support, applicable Stripe processing costs, and other platform operations. Platform fees are not fees for legal services. Payments are securely processed by Stripe. LPC does not store card or payment information.

Common questions

Answers before you get started.

Does LPC assign a paralegal to an attorney?

No. Attorneys review applications and profiles, then decide whether and whom to engage.

Who defines the work and compensation?

The attorney defines the matter’s scope, deliverables, timing, and offered compensation.

Is Let’s-ParaConnect a law firm or staffing agency?

No. LPC is a technology platform. It does not provide legal advice, assign professionals, or supervise legal work.

How are payments handled?

The attorney funds the matter when hiring. Stripe securely processes the payment, and funds are released to the paralegal after the completed work is reviewed and approved.
