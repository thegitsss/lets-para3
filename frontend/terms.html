1. Let’s-ParaConnect Platform Overview

Let’s-ParaConnect is a technology platform designed to allow attorneys to engage independent paralegals for project-based support work. The Platform enables attorneys to post matters and allows approved paralegals to apply for and perform discrete support assignments as independent contractors. Let’s-ParaConnect provides the software infrastructure that facilitates communication, workflow coordination, and payments through the Platform. Attorneys and paralegals decide independently whether to work together through the Platform. Let’s-ParaConnect does not provide legal services, legal representation, or legal advice, is not a law firm, is not a staffing agency, and does not assign engagements. Use of the Platform does not create an attorney-client relationship between Let’s-ParaConnect and any user. The Platform is not a channel for Attorneys to communicate with their own clients and must not be used to receive, transmit, or hold client trust funds.

2. Flat-Fee Matters

2.1 Overview of How Flat-Fee Matters Work

An attorney may post a discrete assignment (a “Matter”) to the Platform. Each Matter posting includes, among other information:

  • a description of the requested work
  • relevant deadlines
  • required tasks
  • the flat-fee amount offered for completion of the Matter

The minimum matter compensation on the Platform is $400.

Approved paralegals may apply to the Matter. When a paralegal applies, their profile is presented to the posting attorney for review. The attorney may review applicants and select a paralegal to perform the work. At that point, the attorney and paralegal are provided access to collaboration tools within the Platform, which may include:

  • secure messaging
  • document sharing
  • matter-related workflow features

2.2 Attorney Payment for Flat-Fee Matters

When an Attorney selects a Paralegal for a flat-fee Matter, the Attorney authorizes payment for the Matter through the Platform. Attorneys must provide valid payment information prior to hiring a Paralegal for a Matter. Payment information is managed through the payment settings section of the Attorney’s account dashboard. By posting and hiring for a flat-fee Matter through the Platform, the Attorney authorizes payment processing through Stripe Connect or another applicable third-party payment processor supported by the Platform. Payments are processed in accordance with the Platform’s payment workflow and the terms of the applicable payment processor.

2.3 Paralegal Payment for Flat-Fee Matters

Upon timely completion of a Matter, the Matter amount presented when the Paralegal applied for or accepted the Matter is the gross compensation basis. The Paralegal platform fee disclosed before application or acceptance is deducted from that gross amount before payout, together with any adjustment authorized through the withdrawal, review, or dispute workflow. The resulting net payout is released to the Paralegal’s designated Stripe Connect account. Payments are processed through the Platform’s payment processor and are released only upon: (i) the hiring Attorney’s confirmation of completion within the Matter dashboard; or (ii) resolution of a dispute or review process administered through the Platform in accordance with the Platform’s payment and workflow policies. Bank-arrival timing varies based on the connected account’s Stripe payout schedule and financial institution; Stripe provides the current payout status and estimated arrival. Let’s-ParaConnect does not take custody of user funds and does not provide banking services. All payment authorization, settlement, and payout timing are governed by the systems and policies of the applicable payment processor.

2.4 Rules Governing the Use of Paralegals for Matter Work

For each Matter posted on the Platform, the Attorney agrees to comply with the following rules governing the use of Paralegals:

  • The Attorney retains sole professional responsibility for all support work and work product associated with the Matter.
  • The Attorney will supervise the Paralegal’s work to ensure compliance with all applicable laws and Rules of Professional Conduct.
  • Unless permitted by applicable professional conduct rules, Paralegals shall not communicate directly with the Attorney’s clients, opposing counsel, witnesses, or other individuals involved in the legal matter.
  • Paralegals shall not appear in court or before any judicial or administrative body on behalf of the Attorney’s client.
  • Paralegals shall not sign or file documents with any court or administrative body on behalf of the Attorney’s client.
  • Where required by law or by the Attorney’s engagement agreement with their client, the Attorney is responsible for obtaining any necessary client consent for the use of paralegal assistance.
  • The Attorney retains sole responsibility for determining the fees charged to the Attorney’s clients for legal services.
  • All payments between Attorneys and Paralegals for work performed through the Platform must be processed through the Let’s-ParaConnect Platform in accordance with these Terms.

2.5 Let’s-ParaConnect Platform Fee for Flat-Fee Matters

Let’s-ParaConnect does not provide legal services and does not charge fees for legal services. In exchange for providing the Platform and related software tools, Let’s-ParaConnect charges separate platform fees to each side of a paid Matter: the Attorney platform fee is added when the Attorney confirms the initial hire, and the Paralegal platform fee is deducted from completed, paid work before payout. The applicable percentages and total amounts are disclosed to each user before the relevant payment or acceptance action. Platform fees are collected and processed through the Platform’s payment processor as part of the Matter payment workflow. Let’s-ParaConnect reserves the right to modify platform fees prospectively. Any changes will apply only to Matters posted after such changes become effective.

3. Paralegal Replacement and Early Termination

3.1 Dispute Review for Flat-Fee Matters

Any dispute relating to a flat-fee Matter must be initiated through the Platform prior to payment release. Let’s-ParaConnect may administer the Platform’s review workflow by collecting information submitted by the Attorney and Paralegal, requesting additional documentation relevant to the Matter, and applying the Platform’s payment and workflow policies. Let’s-ParaConnect does not provide legal advice, does not adjudicate legal claims, and does not act as legal counsel for any user.

3.2 Missed Deadlines

Paralegals are expected to meet all deadlines agreed upon within a Matter unless an extension has been requested and approved by the hiring Attorney through the Platform.

A “missed deadline” occurs when a Paralegal fails to submit the required deliverable by the agreed deadline and has not received approval from the Attorney for an extension.

If a Paralegal misses a deadline and no work product has been delivered, the hiring Attorney may request review through the Platform and may be eligible for a full refund of the Matter amount.

Repeated missed deadlines may result in a manual account review and, where permitted by these Terms and applicable law, a warning, temporary restriction, suspension, or termination of the Paralegal’s account. Let’s-ParaConnect considers the available context rather than applying an automated missed-deadline threshold.

Because deadlines are a material part of Matter work performed through the Platform, unless a Paralegal can establish that the Paralegal was unable to complete a Matter by the agreed deadline due to the Attorney’s inaction, failure to provide necessary information, or other circumstances outside the Paralegal’s control, a dispute based on a missed deadline may be resolved in favor of the Attorney and payment for the Matter may be refunded in full through the Platform’s payment processor.

4. Communication Between Attorneys and Paralegals

Effective communication is essential to successful collaboration on the Platform. By posting a Matter, applying to a Matter, or accepting a Matter, Attorneys and Paralegals agree to communicate professionally and proactively regarding all aspects of the work. Such communication may occur through the Platform’s messaging features or other mutually agreed-upon communication methods and should include, where appropriate:

  • Establishing clear and reasonable deadlines and confirming the ability to meet those deadlines;
  • Clarifying the scope of the Matter and the tasks required to complete the work;
  • Discussing expectations regarding deliverables and work product;
  • Coordinating preferred communication methods and response times; and
  • Sharing any information, documents, or materials necessary to complete the Matter in a timely and professional manner.

Both Attorneys and Paralegals are responsible for maintaining professional communication and promptly responding to reasonable requests for information necessary to complete the Matter. Let’s-ParaConnect provides communication tools within the Platform to facilitate collaboration but does not supervise or control communications between users. If users choose to communicate outside the Platform, they do so at their own discretion, and the Attorney remains responsible for supervision, recordkeeping, and compliance with all applicable professional obligations.
