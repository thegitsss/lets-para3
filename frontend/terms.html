1. Let’s-ParaConnect Platform Overview.

Let’s-ParaConnect is a technology platform designed to allow attorneys to engage independent paralegals for project-based support work. The Platform enables attorneys to post matters and allows approved paralegals to apply for and perform discrete support assignments as independent contractors. Let’s-ParaConnect provides the software infrastructure that facilitates communication, workflow coordination, and payments through the Platform. Attorneys and paralegals decide independently whether to work together through the Platform. Let’s-ParaConnect does not provide legal services, legal representation, or legal advice, is not a law firm, is not a staffing agency, and does not assign engagements. Use of the Platform does not create an attorney-client relationship between Let’s-ParaConnect and any user. The Platform is not a channel for Attorneys to communicate with their own clients and must not be used to receive, transmit, or hold client trust funds.

2. Flat-Fee Matters.

2.1 Overview of How Flat-Fee Matters Work.

An attorney may post a discrete assignment (a “Matter”) to the Platform. Each Matter posting includes, among other information:

  • a description of the requested work
  • relevant deadlines
  • required tasks
  • the flat-fee amount offered for completion of the Matter

The minimum matter compensation on the Platform is $400.

Approved paralegals may apply to the Matter. When a paralegal applies, their profile is presented to the posting attorney for review. The attorney may review applicants and select a paralegal to perform the work. At that point, the attorney and paralegal are provided access to collaboration tools within the Platform, which may include:

  • secure messaging
  • document sharing
  • matter-related workflow features

2.2 Attorney Payment for Flat-Fee Matters.

When an Attorney selects a Paralegal for a flat-fee Matter, the Attorney authorizes payment for the Matter through the Platform. Attorneys must provide valid payment information prior to hiring a Paralegal for a Matter. Payment information is managed through the payment settings section of the Attorney’s account dashboard. By posting and hiring for a flat-fee Matter through the Platform, the Attorney authorizes payment processing through Stripe Connect or another applicable third-party payment processor supported by the Platform. Payments are processed in accordance with the Platform’s payment workflow and the terms of the applicable payment processor.

2.3 Paralegal Payment for Flat-Fee Matters.

The agreed Matter amount is the gross compensation for the work. The Paralegal platform fee described in Section 2.5 is deducted before payout, along with any payment adjustment determined through the applicable withdrawal, review, or dispute process.

The Attorney funds the Matter through Stripe when confirming the hire. LPC instructs Stripe to release the applicable net payment to the Paralegal’s connected account after the Attorney approves completed work or a withdrawal, review, or dispute outcome authorizes payment. LPC may also instruct Stripe to process refunds or other payment adjustments under the Platform’s policies. Payment is not released merely because a deadline has passed.

Stripe processes charges, transfers, refunds, and bank payouts. LPC provides the Platform’s payment instructions; Stripe’s processing requirements and the financial institution determine when funds become available and arrive in a bank account. Paralegals can check Stripe for payout status and estimated arrival. LPC is not a bank, and the Platform must not be used to receive, transmit, or hold client trust funds.

2.4 Rules Governing the Use of Paralegals for Matter Work.

For each Matter posted on the Platform, the Attorney agrees to comply with the following rules governing the use of Paralegals:

  • The Attorney retains sole professional responsibility for all support work and work product associated with the Matter.
  • The Attorney will supervise the Paralegal’s work to ensure compliance with all applicable laws and Rules of Professional Conduct.
  • Unless permitted by applicable professional conduct rules, Paralegals shall not communicate directly with the Attorney’s clients, opposing counsel, witnesses, or other individuals involved in the legal matter.
  • Paralegals shall not appear in court or before any judicial or administrative body on behalf of the Attorney’s client.
  • Paralegals shall not sign or file documents with any court or administrative body on behalf of the Attorney’s client.
  • Where required by law or by the Attorney’s engagement agreement with their client, the Attorney is responsible for obtaining any necessary client consent for the use of paralegal assistance.
  • The Attorney retains sole responsibility for determining the fees charged to the Attorney’s clients for legal services.
  • All payments between Attorneys and Paralegals for work performed through the Platform must be processed through the Let’s-ParaConnect Platform in accordance with these Terms.

2.5 Let’s-ParaConnect Platform Fee for Flat-Fee Matters.

LPC charges separate platform fees to Attorneys and Paralegals for paid Matter work:

  • Attorney platform fee: 22%. This fee is added to the Matter amount when the Attorney confirms the initial hire. A $400 Matter has an $88 Attorney platform fee, for a total charge of $488.
  • Paralegal platform fee: 18%. This fee is deducted from the gross compensation payable to the Paralegal before payout. For completed work on a $400 Matter, the fee is $72 and the net payout is $328, before any applicable payment adjustment.

The Platform displays the applicable fees and amounts before the relevant payment or acceptance action. No separate Stripe-processing line item is added beyond the total shown at confirmation. Where a partial payment is authorized, the Paralegal platform fee applies to the gross amount of that payment.

Platform fees support application review, the Matter workspace, platform support, applicable Stripe processing costs, and LPC operations. They are not fees for legal services. Refunds and payment adjustments depend on the Matter’s funding status, work completed, and applicable review outcome; they are not automatic.

LPC may change platform fees prospectively. Any change will apply only to Matters posted after the change takes effect, and the applicable fees will be disclosed before the relevant payment or acceptance action.

3. Paralegal Replacement and Early Termination.

If work needs to end early, use the cancellation, termination, withdrawal, or review option available in the Matter workspace. The available options depend on whether someone has been hired, the Matter’s funding status, and the work completed. Ending work does not automatically cancel a payment or create a right to a refund.

3.1 Canceling Before Hiring.

An Attorney may delete an open Matter posting before hiring if it has no payment or dispute history. An application or accepted invitation does not create an engagement. If the Matter has payment activity or a prior engagement, use the available review options or contact LPC for assistance.

3.2 Ending an Engagement After Hiring.

An Attorney who needs to end an active engagement may request termination in the Matter workspace and explain the reason. A termination request opens a review; it does not automatically issue a refund or settle what the Paralegal is owed.

The Matter is placed under review, and the Paralegal’s regular workspace access is revoked. Both parties should preserve relevant messages, files, and work product and respond to requests made through the review process. Do not continue work while the engagement is under termination review unless the Platform confirms that work may resume.

3.3 Paralegal Withdrawal.

A Paralegal who cannot continue should tell the Attorney promptly and use the Matter’s withdrawal option. Withdrawal is available before all required tasks are complete. Completed work and any payment concerns should be addressed through the completion or review process instead.

If no tasks have been completed, withdrawal results in no payout and the Matter is relisted. If some tasks have been completed, the Attorney reviews the completed work and may authorize a partial payout or decline payment. A partial payout is based on the work completed and is not automatically earned by marking a task complete.

3.4 Requesting Review of a Withdrawal Decision.

If the Attorney declines a partial payout after withdrawal, the Paralegal has 24 hours to request review through the Matter. The deadline is shown in the Platform. If no review is requested before that deadline, the withdrawal is finalized with no payout and any remaining Matter funds become available for replacement work.

A timely review request keeps the payment decision under review. Replacement hiring must wait until the applicable review and payment decision are resolved. A review deadline is not a promise that LPC will resolve the issue or that funds will reach a bank account within that time.

3.5 Payments, Refunds, and Platform Fees.

The payment outcome depends on the Matter’s funding status, the agreed scope, work completed, prior payments, and any review decision. An outcome may include no payout, a partial payout, payment for completed work, or a refund of eligible funds. Refunds and payment adjustments are not automatic.

The Paralegal platform fee described in Section 2.5 is deducted from any gross compensation approved for payment, including a partial payment. Any refund of the Matter amount or Attorney platform fee is determined through the applicable payment or review process. A termination or withdrawal request alone does not refund either amount.

After withdrawal, funds remaining for replacement work stay allocated to the Matter unless a refund is separately approved. They are not automatically returned to the Attorney. LPC sends the applicable payment or refund instructions to Stripe; processing and bank-arrival timing follow Section 2.3.

3.6 Relisting and Choosing a Replacement.

A Matter may be relisted when the withdrawal and payment decision are finalized, any applicable review window has ended, and funds remain for the unfinished work. The remaining budget reflects any gross compensation already allocated to completed work.

The Attorney reviews applicants and chooses whom to engage for the remaining work. LPC does not assign a replacement or guarantee that another Paralegal will be available. Review the remaining scope, tasks, deadline, and compensation before confirming a replacement hire. Additional work outside the agreed scope requires a new Matter.

3.7 Missed Deadlines.

Paralegals are expected to meet the agreed Matter deadline. If a delay is likely, tell the Attorney promptly and document any agreed extension in the Matter workspace.

If work is late or has not been delivered, the Attorney should raise the issue through the Matter’s review process before payment is released. LPC considers the agreed scope, delivered work, communications, any agreed extension, and circumstances that affected completion. A missed deadline does not automatically determine the payment outcome.

If no work has been delivered, the Attorney may be eligible for a full refund following review. Repeated missed deadlines may lead to a manual account review and, where permitted by these Terms and applicable law, a warning, restriction, suspension, or termination.

3.8 What to Include in a Review Request.

Raise work or payment concerns in the applicable Matter before payment is released and within any review deadline shown. Include the agreed scope, relevant messages, delivered work, and a clear explanation of the issue. Both parties must cooperate with reasonable requests for information.

LPC reviews the Platform’s payment and workflow issue and may request additional information before deciding the outcome. LPC does not provide legal advice or decide legal claims between users. See Section 11 for the general Matter review process.
